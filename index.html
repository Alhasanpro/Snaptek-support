Frequently asked questions

Tap the question that matches your situation.

What is Snaptek?

Snaptek is an iOS app that turns screenshots, places, profiles, and links into clean, searchable cards. It reads what's inside an image, organizes it into folders automatically, and connects it to actions like "open in Maps" or "save contact."

How does AI processing work?

When you capture or import an image, Snaptek runs OCR + entity detection on-device to extract text, usernames, phone numbers, addresses, and links. It then enriches the result — for example turning a restaurant name into a Maps link — and assigns the card to the right folder.

Why didn't my screenshot land in the right folder?

Auto-classification picks the most likely folder based on what was detected. If it gets it wrong, long-press the card and choose Move to folder — Snaptek learns from your overrides.

You can also create a folder manually from the Folders tab using the + button in the top-right.

What is iCloud Sync, and how do I turn it on?

iCloud Sync is a Pro feature that keeps your cards and folders synced across your iPhone, iPad, and other Apple devices signed into the same iCloud account.

Enable it from Settings → iCloud Sync. The first sync hydrates progressively — large libraries may take a few minutes to fully appear.

Smart Search isn't finding old screenshots

Indexing runs in the background and pauses when your iPhone is locked or low on battery. Plug in, leave the app open, and pull-to-refresh on the Recent tab to kick it forward.

Searching by visible text, tags, categories, and locations is supported.

Permissions — what does Snaptek ask for, and why?

  • Photos — to import and process the screenshots you choose.
  • Camera — so you can capture new content directly inside the app.
  • Location — optional, used to attach a place to a card and power city-based folders.
  • Notifications — optional, used for reminders tied to saved content (e.g. "you saved this restaurant nearby").

You can grant or revoke any of these any time in iOS Settings → Snaptek.

Delete a card or folder

Swipe a card left and tap Delete. To delete a folder, open it, tap the ••• menu, then Delete folder. Cards inside a deleted folder return to Recent — they're not erased unless you delete them too.

Privacy — where does my data go?

Image recognition runs on your device. With iCloud Sync off, nothing leaves your iPhone. With iCloud Sync on, your cards and folders are stored in your private iCloud container — not on Snaptek servers.

Restore purchases on a new device

Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to buy Snaptek Pro, open Settings → Restore Purchases, and your subscription / lifetime unlock will reappear.

Privacy policy

Designed with privacy at its core. Your data stays on your device.

Last updated · April 26, 2026

Overview

Snaptek is designed with your privacy at its core. We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide the app's features. Your screenshots, cards, and personal data remain on your device unless you explicitly enable iCloud sync.

Data we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple ID identifier and, optionally, your name and email. We do not share this information with third parties.
  • Photos and camera. Images you capture or import are processed entirely on your device using on-device OCR. We never upload your images to external servers.
  • Location. When enabled, your approximate location is captured at the time of scanning to add place context to cards. Location data is stored locally on your device.
  • Extracted text and metadata. OCR text, detected links, tags, and social media information are generated and stored locally on your device.

iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync (Pro feature), your cards, folders, and associated data are synced through your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit framework. This data is protected by Apple's iCloud security and encryption. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

On-device processing

All image analysis, text recognition (OCR), social media detection, and content extraction happen entirely on your device. No images or extracted content are sent to external servers or AI services.

Third-party services

Snaptek does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking services. We do not sell, share, or monetize your data in any way.

Data storage & deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If iCloud sync is enabled, a copy is stored in your personal iCloud account. You can delete any card or folder at any time. Signing out removes your local account data. Deleting the app removes all local data permanently.

Permissions

Snaptek requests the following permissions, each for a specific purpose:

  • Camera — to capture photos for creating cards.
  • Photo Library — to import existing screenshots and images.
  • Location — to add place context to captured cards.

You can revoke any permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Children's privacy

Snaptek is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the app with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
